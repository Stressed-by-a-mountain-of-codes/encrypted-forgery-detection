# Dataset 07: Korus 2018 — Copy-Move & Splicing with JPEG Variants

**Full Name:** Korus 2018 Image Forgery Dataset  
**Authors:** Korus, Dziech (Polish-Japanese Academy)  
**Year:** 2018  
**Website:** https://github.com/pkorus/datasets  

---

## My Walkthrough

I went through this dataset because it's **designed specifically for JPEG forensics** — 1,600 images with **controlled JPEG quality factors** and **double compression**. Created to test compression-based detectors (like Comprint, FF-Net, PP-JPEG).

Composition: **400 authentic** (RAW from 4 cameras), **1,200 forged** — **Copy-Move** (600) and **Splicing** (600). Each forgery saved at **multiple QFs**: {70, 75, 80, 85, 90, 95} + **double JPEG** (QF1=90 → QF2=75). Ground truth: **binary masks** + **QF labels** + **camera model**.

Key value: **Controlled compression**. You know exact QF, single vs double compression. Perfect for testing **double-JPEG detectors** (FF-Net, PP-JPEG) and **compression fingerprint** methods (Comprint). The authentic RAW→JPEG pipeline gives clean camera fingerprints.

On the 2nd iteration: **No removal/inpainting/DeepFake**. Only copy-move and splicing. **Cameras: 4 models** (Canon, Nikon, Sony, Panasonic) — limited diversity. But for **JPEG forensics research**, this is a **gold standard**. Paper 12 (PP-JPEG) would love this dataset.

For encrypted-domain: **Perfect for PP-JPEG replication** — known QFs, known double-compression. Encrypt at each QF, test energy graph minima detection. Camera fingerprint in encrypted domain (Comprint style).

**My takeaway:** Korus 2018 = **JPEG forensics gold standard**. Essential if your encrypted method uses compression artifacts (PP-JPEG, LEFN, Comprint). Not for general forgery detection (limited types), but **unbeatable for compression-controlled experiments**.

## Constraints & Gotchas

- ❌ **Only copy-move + splicing** — no removal, inpainting, DeepFake
- ❌ **Only 4 cameras** — limited fingerprint diversity
- ❌ **Large download** (~3 GB)
- ❌ **No official train/test split**
- ✅ **Controlled QF + double JPEG** — gold standard for JPEG forensics
- ✅ **Known ground truth QF** — perfect for PP-JPEG/Comprint eval
- ✅ **Camera labels** — encrypted fingerprint test
- ✅ **Used by JPEG forensics papers** — direct comparison
